        Filter and sort

        Searching

        Words and phrases

        lighting rental
        Records with either word. The best matches come first; small typos still match.
        "exact phrase"
        Double quotes: the whole phrase must appear.
        -word -"a phrase"
        Leaves out records that contain it.
        Exact
        The switch in the search box matches the text letter for letter. It turns on by itself for request numbers and project codes.

        Field filters

        Type / in the search box to pick a field, then type a value. Filters become chips and combine with each other and with the words you type.

        project_code:'2026TEG'
        Single quotes: the field contains the text.
        project_code:"2026TEG-P0290"
        Double quotes: the field is exactly the text.
        Any value
        Suggestions appear once you type, but any text works.
        Click a chip
        Switches it off (grey) without removing it. Click again to switch it back on.
        Double-click a chip
        Edits its value. F2 does the same from the keyboard.
        Type them
        A typed field:'value' becomes a chip when you press Enter.

        particular_item business_unit project_name project_code project_manager supplier_account_name

        Tabs, filters and sort

        Tabs
        Search only the items (Particulars), the suppliers, or the projects. The counts follow the filters.
        Line Items
        The same search over the lines of past cost estimates (CEs): what each line budgeted, its markup and client price, which CE and tab it's from, and what was actually paid. Click a purchase order number in a line to search for it. The tab shows when the line item service is available.
        Line Items filters
        On the Line Items tab, the funnel button has its own filters: final CE only (or other versions only), project outcome, category, and with or without a matched purchase order; sort by price date, unit cost, line cost, markup or project code; group by project, category or business unit, with a total per group.
        Filter and sort
        The funnel button: sort by date, price, unit price or project code; filter by status, outsourcing and currency; group by business unit, project or supplier, with a total per group.
        Status
        Only Approved records are shown until you pick more.
        Browse all
        Every record, newest first, with the same filters, sort and groups.
        Prices
        Settings > Prices: the exchange rate (57 PHP per USD unless you change it) and the currency prices are shown in.

        Sharing a search

        The address bar always holds the search, the tab, the filters and the record you have open. Copy it to share exactly what you see. The person you send it to logs in with Lark first and sees only what their account can open.

        ?q=
        The search, field filters included. ?qoff= holds the chips that are switched off.
        ?exact=true
        Exact matching.
        ?tab=
        particulars, suppliers or projects.
        ?sort=
        datedesc, dateasc, pricedesc, priceasc, unitpriceasc, unitpricedesc, projaz, projza.
        ?status=
        Comma separated: approved,under review,recalled,rejected.
        ?outsource=
        show (only outsourced) or hide.
        ?currency=
        php or usd.
        ?group=
        business unit, project or supplier.
        ?all=true
        Browse all.
        ?exchangerate= ?showprice=
        PHP per 1 USD (e.g. 57), and php, usd or default.
        ?theme=
        light, dark or system.

        The records

        Suppliers reads the procurement table in Lark Base each time it opens, as you. While it reads, the copy saved from your last visit is already on screen (“Saved copy from …”), without bank details. The line under the tabs says when it last read the table (“Updated 4:44 PM”). Refresh reads it again now. Coming back to the tab after half an hour refreshes it quietly; if results are on screen, they stay put until you choose Show them.

        Keyboard

        /
        Jumps to the search box (and, in it, opens the field list).
        ↑ ↓ Enter Tab
        Move through the field list and pick one; Enter also finishes a filter's value.
        Backspace
        In an empty search box, removes the last chip.
        Esc
        Closes the field list, then cancels the chip being typed, then clears the search. Outside the box it closes the record.
